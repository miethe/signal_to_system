- **Composition / positioning:** dark field; central luminous contract record; four labelled rails enter from cardinal directions and terminate in a sealed boundary around it; title upper-left.
- **Layout:** `INTENT ↓` / `ACCEPTANCE → [ CONTRACT ] ← AUTHORITY` / `EVIDENCE ↑`.
- **Scheme / variant / master:** signal-dark / single / 2400×1350 (16:9).
- **Alt:** A central contract record is bound to intent, acceptance, evidence, and authority.
- **Caption:** A contract is the structure that makes delegated work governable.

- **Acceptance checks:** central record dominates; all four rails are labelled; no decorative filler.
