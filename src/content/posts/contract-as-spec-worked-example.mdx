*This extends the inline `fc-example-041` sketch in ["The Contract Is the Work"](/essays/the-contract-is-the-work) into a complete
worked instance: claims bound to evidence, verifier status, a visible amendment, and the final
acceptance decision. It is entirely synthetic, with no real identifiers, no real dollar figures, no
real names or paths. It exists to show the shape a completed contract-as-spec artifact actually
takes, not to document a real incident.*

```
Feature Contract: fc-example-041
Status: accepted

Intent:
  Reduce checkout-timeout errors for a specific request class to near zero.

Acceptance criteria:
  1. The failure mode does not recur under the documented repro conditions.
  2. The existing test suite passes with no reduction in coverage.
  3. The fix is traceable to a specific root cause, not a symptom suppression.
  4. A rollback path exists and is documented.

Evidence obligations:
  - Root-cause analysis, with the failing and passing runs both attached.
  - Independent verification that criterion 1 holds outside the original repro environment.

Decision authority: the on-call engineering lead accepts or rejects the completed contract.
```

## Claims bound to evidence

The contract has one consequential claim, criterion 1 (the failure mode does not recur), for
the reason stated in criterion 3 (a root cause, not a suppression). Criteria 2 and 4 are
checklist obligations rather than mechanism claims: they get a pass/fail evidence item each,
not a full claim-binding treatment, because there is no mechanism to explain, only a state to
confirm. The claim and its evidence are recorded together, not left to be inferred from a
narrative summary:

```
Claim: The timeout no longer recurs under the documented repro conditions, because
       the retry logic no longer double-acquires the connection-pool lock.
       (supports: criterion 1, criterion 3)

Bound evidence:
  - ev-01  root-cause-trace.log        kind: reproduction      verifier: pending   supports: criterion 3
  - ev-02  fixed-run-repro.log         kind: reproduction      verifier: verified  supports: criterion 1
  - ev-03  independent-repro-env.log   kind: independent_run   verifier: verified  supports: criterion 1
  - ev-04  pool-lock-diff.patch        kind: root_cause_link   verifier: verified  supports: criterion 3
  - ev-05  test-suite-run.log          kind: checklist         verifier: verified  supports: criterion 2
  - ev-06  rollback-runbook.md         kind: checklist         verifier: verified  supports: criterion 4

Verifier status: 5 of 6 evidence items verified; ev-01 pending an independent read
of the trace, corroborating (not blocking) the root cause already established by ev-04.
```

The claim is not "the fix works": that sentence has no evidence obligation attached to it and
could mean almost anything. The claim states the specific mechanism, and each evidence item
carries an explicit `supports:` field naming the criterion it ties to, so a reviewer can check
the claim against the evidence directly rather than against the surrounding prose or trust that
an unlabeled item happens to cover the criterion it is invoked for.

## A visible amendment

Partway through execution, the assigned specialist found that criterion 2 as written ("no
reduction in coverage") could not distinguish a genuine regression from an unrelated flaky test
already failing on `main`. The specialist did not quietly reinterpret the criterion. It proposed
an amendment, and someone other than the specialist accepted it:

```
Amendment 1
  Date: 2026-06-14 (synthetic)
  Proposed by: specialist-implementation-leg-1 (fc-example-041)
  Proposed change: criterion 2 becomes "the existing test suite passes with no reduction in
    coverage, excluding tests already failing on the base commit before this change began"
  Reason: the unqualified criterion could not be satisfied or refuted; a pre-existing flake on
    the base commit made "no reduction" undecidable as originally worded (base-commit test
    result attached alongside ev-05 as amendment-supporting evidence, not a numbered claim item).
  Accepted by: on-call-lead-1
  Accepted: yes, the amendment narrows the criterion's ambiguity without weakening its intent
```

The amendment is visible as a dated, attributed change to the contract itself, not as a footnote
in a completion report. The party proposing it and the party accepting it are different people:
the specialist executing against the contract cannot also be the one who decides the contract now
reads differently.

## Final acceptance decision

```
Decision: accepted
Decided by: on-call-lead-1
Basis: all four acceptance criteria met under the amended contract (ev-02/ev-03 satisfy
  criterion 1; ev-04 independently satisfies criterion 3's root-cause claim; ev-05 satisfies
  criterion 2 under the amendment; ev-06 satisfies criterion 4); 5 of 6 bound evidence items
  independently verified; ev-01 is corroborating, not required to close criterion 3, since ev-04
  already establishes the root cause on its own.
Reusable artifact: not promoted, since this fix was scoped to one request class and is not yet
  a candidate for stage 6 (reusable capability).
```

The decision names what was checked, what remained unresolved, and why the unresolved item did
not block acceptance. A reader six months later does not have to trust that judgment; they can
see the same evidence record (each item's kind, verifier status, and the criterion it supports)
and reach their own.
