Some projects start as small tools for a specific problem. Others are places to investigate a question or applications that put an idea in someone's hands. This guide groups the public work by what it is for.

## Mechanisms

Mechanisms are focused tools for a particular job. [Agent Context Budget](https://github.com/miethe/agent-mechanisms/tree/main/agent-context-budget) measures instruction files against a configurable byte budget and reports where a chain exceeds it. It is a way to inspect files, not a promise about what every agent will read.

## Labs

Labs are spaces for experiments. [Aural Geometry Lab](https://github.com/miethe/aural-geometry-lab) explores mathematical ideas through sound, while [MagnaMath Lab](https://github.com/miethe/magnamath-lab) explores polyhedra, nets, and folding. Research notes on this site document questions, methods, and limits alongside results.

## Products

Products bring a set of capabilities together for repeated use. [MeatyCapture](https://github.com/miethe/meatycapture) is a task management tool with command-line, web, and desktop interfaces. [CCDash](https://github.com/miethe/CCDash) is a dashboard for agentic AI sessions.

The [portfolio](/portfolio/) collects more projects, and [writing](/writing/) follows the ideas and decisions behind them. This guide can grow as more work is reviewed and made public.
