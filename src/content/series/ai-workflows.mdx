AI work breaks down when every task gets pushed through the same chat window. Research, planning, execution, review, visual production, and knowledge retention each need different artifacts and different routing decisions.

This series documents the workflow-first operating model I use across those tasks. The emphasis is not tool fandom. It is the decision logic underneath the tools: sensitivity, first artifact required, speed versus depth, cost, and reusability.

The core pattern is simple: route work to the system best suited to produce the first durable artifact. Once that artifact exists, the rest of the workflow can inherit it, validate against it, and reuse it later.
